Spring 0–120 m, then summer storm 120–240 m (lightning lanes and rain puddles), then autumn leaf wind 240–360 m (gust pairs and falling branches), then endless winter from 360 m (ice lanes and snow drifts). Swipe to change lanes, tap or press Space to jump. Three gray lanes, original hopper, no ads.

How to play

  • Change lanes: swipe left or right, or press ← / → or A / D.
  • Jump: tap, or press Space or ↑.
  • Climb as high as you can. Your score is the best height you reach this run, in whole metres.
  • The bottom keeps rising. Touch it and the run ends.
  • One Catch per run. On your first fall you can use it to go back to your last safe spot in a clear lane, then press Ready when you are set. It is not offered if the bottom is too close. No checkpoints.
  • Pause: the II button at the top centre, or Esc / P.

Caught

First fall this run. Use Catch to restore your last safe height, or end the run. No timer and no ad.

Ready?

Paused

The climb is frozen. Nothing moves until you resume, and a short countdown runs first.
